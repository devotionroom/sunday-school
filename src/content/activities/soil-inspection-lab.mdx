### Purpose
To give children a tactile, hands-on experience feeling the four distinct surfaces from Jesus' parable, explaining why seeds can only flourish when the soil is soft, deep, and clean.

### Setup
1. Arrange the four plates side by side on a table in front of the class.
2. Label each plate: 1. The Hard Path, 2. Rocky Ground, 3. Thorny Patch, 4. Good Soil.
3. Have a handful of seeds ready.

### Instructions
1. Invite several children to come up and touch each of the four plates with their fingers:
   - *"How does Plate 1 feel? Hard as concrete! If a seed falls here, can it get inside? No, birds will snatch it."*
   - *"How does Plate 2 feel? Bumpy rocks! A seed might sprout, but can roots dig deep through rock? No, the sun will scorch it."*
   - *"How does Plate 3 feel? Sharp, prickly weeds! What happens when weeds drink all the water and block the sunlight? The plant gets choked out."*
   - *"How does Plate 4 feel? Soft, cool, and rich! A seed here can sink its roots deep, drink rain, and produce an abundant crop."*
2. Drop seeds onto each plate and explain: *"The seed is God's Word. It is always good. But what kind of soil is your heart today?"*

### Age Adaptations
<AgeGroup level="preschool">
Let every child touch the hard stone and say *"Hard path!"*, then feel the soft dark soil and say *"Good soil!"* Teach them to ask Jesus to make their hearts soft like the potting soil.
</AgeGroup>

<AgeGroup level="upper-elementary">
Ask older kids to identify specific "weeds" in a 5th grader's life (screen time addiction, stressing over grades, wanting to be popular) and discuss practical ways to "weed the garden" of their hearts.
</AgeGroup>
