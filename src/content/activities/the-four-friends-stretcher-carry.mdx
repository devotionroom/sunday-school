### Purpose
To demonstrate the teamwork, persistence, and deep love it took for four friends to carry a paralyzed man across town and through obstacles to reach Jesus.

### Setup
1. Set up an obstacle course in the room with chairs, cones, or tables clustered close together to represent the packed crowd at Capernaum where no one could get through.
2. Form teams of four children.
3. Give each team a towel or blanket, with each child holding one corner.
4. Place a stuffed animal in the center of the blanket.

### Instructions
1. When the teacher calls "Go!", the four friends must work together to carry the stuffed animal through the obstacle course without letting it fall off the blanket.
2. If the animal falls off, the team must freeze, gently put the animal back on the mat, and take three steps back before continuing.
3. The team reaches the "Roof Station" at the end of the room and gently lowers their blanket down to the floor in front of a picture or cross representing Jesus.
4. Celebrate together: *"They made it to Jesus!"*

### Age Adaptations
<AgeGroup level="preschool">
Have the four children hold hands in a circle around the teacher who holds the mat, walking together through an open hallway while chanting: *"One, two, three, four friends! Jesus helps us!"*
</AgeGroup>

<AgeGroup level="upper-elementary">
Make the course more challenging by narrowing the pathways or requiring the four carriers to communicate without speaking aloud (using only head nods and hand motions to coordinate balance).
</AgeGroup>
