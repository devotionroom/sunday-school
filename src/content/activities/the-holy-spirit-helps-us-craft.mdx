### Purpose
To teach children that on the day of Pentecost, Jesus sent the Holy Spirit with rushing wind and tongues of fire to dwell within His followers, filling them with power, peace, and courage to share the good news.

### Finished Craft Reference
![The Holy Spirit Helps Us Craft](/sunday-school/crafts/pentecost-flames.jpg)

### Setup
1. Print the craft template on white paper or cardstock for each child.
2. Provide each child with one sheet of blue construction paper (placed horizontally in landscape orientation).
3. Set out crayons, scissors, and glue sticks. Pre-cut the small flame cutouts for children aged 5–7.

### Instructions
1. **Color All Pieces:**
   - The Disciples in the Room (color their robes in varied colors: red, blue, green, brown, gold, and the stone room walls).
   - The Individual Flame Shapes (color in fiery gradients of bright red, orange, and yellow).
   - The Title Banner (*"THE HOLY SPIRIT HELPS US!"*).
2. **Cut Out:** Carefully cut out the stone room scene with the disciples, the flame shapes, and the title banner along the dashed cut lines.
3. **Glue the Room Scene:** Glue the group of praying disciples in the lower-center of the blue construction paper.
4. **Glue the Flames:** Glue one individual flame directly above the head of each disciple to represent the tongues of fire resting upon them.
5. **Add the Title Banner:** Glue the *"THE HOLY SPIRIT HELPS US!"* banner centered across the top of the paper.

### Discussion While Crafting
- *"What two amazing signs happened when the Holy Spirit arrived at Pentecost? (Sound of rushing wind and tongues of fire!)"*
- *"How did the Holy Spirit change Peter from scared to bold?"*
- *"Does the Holy Spirit still help us today? (Yes! He gives us courage to love others and tell people about Jesus)."*
