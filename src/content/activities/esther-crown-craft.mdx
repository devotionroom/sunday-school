### Purpose

To give children a hands-on reminder of Esther's courage and royal position. Children colour and assemble a "BE BRAVE!" crown — a wearable take-home that links Esther's story to their own call to courage.

### Template

The printed template includes:
- A crown shape with **"BE BRAVE!"** in large letters across the front
- Diamond shapes and gem circle designs to colour
- Stars at the top points of the crown
- Two Headband Strips to cut out and attach to fit the crown around the child's head

![Be Brave! Esther's Crown](/sunday-school/crafts/esther-crown-new.jpg)

### Setup

Print one template per child on white cardstock. For children aged 5–6, the teacher pre-cuts along the dashed lines before class to save time.

### Instructions

1. **Colour the crown:** Colour the entire crown bright yellow or gold using crayons. Colour the letters **"BE BRAVE!"** in bright colours — make each letter a different colour for extra fun!

2. **Colour the gems:** Colour the diamond shapes and circle gems in bold colours — red, blue, green, and purple. Press firmly so the colours are vivid.

3. **Add stickers (optional):** Press adhesive gem stickers onto the circle positions on the crown for a sparkly, royal finish. This optional step adds a lovely tactile element and children love it.

4. **Cut out the pieces:** Cut the crown and both headband strips along the dashed lines. For children aged 5–6, the teacher should pre-cut these before class.

5. **Assemble:** Wrap the crown around the child's head to measure the right size. Attach one or both headband strips to the sides of the crown with glue or tape until it fits snugly.

6. **Wear it!** Children wear their crowns and the teacher reminds them: *Esther was brave because she trusted God — and so can we!*

### Discussion While Crafting

Ask children as they colour and cut:

- *"What does 'Be Brave!' mean for Esther's story?"*
- *"What made Esther brave enough to go to the king — was it because she wasn't scared, or because she trusted God even though she was scared?"*
- *"God turned Haman's plan completely upside down! What do we call that celebration?"* (Purim!)
- *"Can you think of a time when you needed to be brave? Who can help you be brave like Esther?"*

<AgeGroup level="preschool">
### For Younger Children

Focus on the colouring — preschoolers may need help cutting and assembling. Let them choose their favourite colours freely. When their crown is on, give them a simple phrase to repeat: *"I can be brave because God is with me!"*
</AgeGroup>

<AgeGroup level="upper-elementary">
### For Older Children

As older children work, invite them to write **"For Such a Time as This"** (Esther 4:14) in small letters inside the crown band, alongside "BE BRAVE!" — a reminder that their own brave moments are part of God's plan too.
</AgeGroup>
