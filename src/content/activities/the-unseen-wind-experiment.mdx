### Purpose
To demonstrate how an invisible force (wind) can be unseen by human eyes, yet produce undeniable, powerful, moving results—just like the Holy Spirit working in our hearts.

### Setup
1. Have the pinwheel or feather resting on a table in front of the class.
2. Have the fan or piece of cardboard ready.

### Instructions
1. Ask the children: *"Can anyone see the air in this room? What color is it? Does it have a shape?"*
2. Wave the fan or blow the cool hair dryer toward the pinwheel. Watch it spin with incredible speed!
3. Blow air toward the children's faces and hands. Ask: *"Can you see the wind? No! But can you feel it? Can you see what it moves?"*
4. Explain: *"In John 3 and Acts 2, Jesus compares the Holy Spirit to the wind. You cannot see Him with your physical eyes, but when He enters a life, everything changes! Fear turns to courage, sadness turns to joy, and people are empowered to tell the world about Jesus."*

### Age Adaptations
<AgeGroup level="preschool">
Give each child a small piece of tissue paper. Have them cup it in their hands, take a giant breath, and blow it into the air, watching it flutter down while saying: *"The Spirit gives us breath and power!"*
</AgeGroup>

<AgeGroup level="upper-elementary">
Discuss how sails on a ship work. The boat cannot move on its own by wishing; it must unfurl its sails to catch the wind. In the same way, we must "set our sails" to the Holy Spirit through prayer and obedience.
</AgeGroup>
