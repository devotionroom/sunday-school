### Purpose
To show children that Jesus' tomb was found physically empty, and to introduce the joyful gospel reality that Jesus conquered death and is alive today.

### Setup
Before class, place the note that reads **"HE IS NOT HERE. HE IS RISEN."** inside the small box or bag. Place the rock or ball firmly in front of the opening of the box so that the "tomb" is sealed shut. Set it on a table in front of the classroom where every child can see it.

### Instructions
1. **Show the sealed tomb:** Point to the box and the stone. Explain: *"After Jesus died on the cross, His friends lovingly placed His body in a tomb carved out of solid rock. Then a huge, heavy stone was rolled across the entrance to seal it tight. Roman guards stood watch."*
2. **Invite a volunteer:** Ask a volunteer to come up to the front and "roll away the stone" from the entrance of the box.
3. **Look inside:** Have the child look inside the box and discover the note.
4. **Read the message aloud:** Read the words together as a class: **"HE IS NOT HERE. HE IS RISEN."**
5. **Connect to Scripture:** Ask: *"When the women went to the tomb on Sunday morning, what did they find?"* Turn to Luke 24:5–6 and Matthew 28:5–6. Explain that the angel told them: *"Why do you seek the living among the dead? He is not here; He has risen!"*
6. **Key Insight:** Explain to the children: *"The stone was not rolled away to let Jesus out — He had already risen by the power of God! The stone was rolled away so the disciples could look inside and see with their own eyes that the tomb was empty."*

<AgeGroup level="preschool">
### For Younger Children
Use very simple words and dramatic actions:
- *"Jesus truly died on the cross for us."*
- *"They laid Him in a dark cave called a tomb."*
- *"Then God raised Jesus back to life!"*
- *"When the women went to the tomb on Sunday morning — it was EMPTY!"*
Have all the preschoolers shout together with big smiles: **"He is alive!"**
</AgeGroup>

<AgeGroup level="upper-elementary">
### For Older Children
Discuss what **"raised on the third day"** means according to Jewish timekeeping and Old Testament prophecy (Hosea 6:2; Jonah 1:17; Matthew 12:40; 1 Corinthians 15:4). Discuss why the empty tomb is foundational historical evidence of the resurrection:
- The tomb was guarded by Roman soldiers and sealed with an official seal.
- If Jesus' enemies had His body, they would have displayed it to stop the Christian message.
- If His disciples had stolen the body, they would not have willingly suffered and died for what they knew to be a lie.
- The empty tomb stands as historical proof that Jesus bodily defeated death.
</AgeGroup>

### Discussion While Reflecting
- *"Why do you think the stone was rolled away if Jesus had already risen?"*
- *"What did the women expect to find at the tomb, and what did they actually find?"*
- *"How does knowing Jesus is alive change how we feel when we are sad or afraid?"*
