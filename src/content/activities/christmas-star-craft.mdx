### Purpose
To give children a bright, memorable take-home reminder that God kept His promise at Christmas — and that He always keeps His promises.

### Setup
![God Keeps His Promises Star](/sunday-school/crafts/christmas-star.jpg)

Print one Christmas Star Template per child on white cardstock or yellow paper. Pre-cut the stars for children aged 5–6. Prepare strips of paper with "God Keeps His Promises" written in dark marker (one per child), or have older children copy it themselves.

### Instructions
1. **Colour the star:** Colour the printed star shape bright yellow using crayons. Add extra decorations — small stars, dots, and swirls — using orange and gold crayons.
2. **Cut it out:** Cut the star along the dashed cut line. For children aged 5–6, the teacher pre-cuts the star before class.
3. **Add the label:** Write or glue a strip of paper across the centre of the star with *"God Keeps His Promises"* written on it in dark marker.
4. **Display:** Hold up your star and say the memory verse together: *"Today in the city of David a Savior has been born to you. He is Christ the Lord." — Luke 2:11*

<AgeGroup level="preschool">
For younger children, pre-cut the stars and pre-glue the label strip. Let them focus on colouring with big, bright strokes. Hold up all the stars together and say: *"God made a promise — and God kept it!"*
</AgeGroup>

### Discussion While Crafting
- *"How long did God's people wait for the Saviour to come?"*
- *"Did God forget His promise?"* (No!)
- *"What promise did God keep at Christmas?"*
