### Purpose
To help children see that God made many specific promises about the coming Saviour long before Jesus was born — and that every single one came true at Christmas.

### Setup
Before class, write one prophecy or promise on each paper strip. Suggested promises:
- "Born in Bethlehem" (Micah 5:2)
- "Born of a virgin" (Isaiah 7:14)
- "Called Emmanuel — God with us" (Isaiah 7:14)
- "Called a Wonderful Counsellor, Prince of Peace" (Isaiah 9:6)
- "Angels announced His birth" (Luke 2:10–11)
- "Shepherds came to worship Him" (Luke 2:15–16)

For preschoolers, draw a simple picture on each strip instead of words (e.g. a star, a baby, a town).

### Instructions
1. **Introduce the idea:** Tell children that hundreds of years before Jesus was born, God made promises about exactly how and where the Saviour would come. These are called prophecies.
2. **Read a strip:** Read the first promise aloud. Ask: *"Did God keep this promise?"* Then loop the strip and secure it to form the first chain link.
3. **Keep going:** Read each strip one at a time. Each time, the child helps attach a new link to the growing chain.
4. **Hold up the finished chain:** Ask: *"Did God keep ALL of these promises?"* (Yes!)
5. **Lead in:** *"Today we are going to hear the story of how God kept every one of His promises — at Christmas."*

<AgeGroup level="preschool">
For younger children, hold the strips up and name the picture on each one. Let each child attach one link. Emphasise: *"God said it, and God did it!"*
</AgeGroup>

### Discussion While Crafting
- *"Why do you think God made so many promises before Jesus was born?"*
- *"If God kept all these promises, what does that tell us about God?"*
- *"Can you think of a promise someone has kept for you?"*
