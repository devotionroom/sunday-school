## Finished Craft

The completed craft shows a **blue construction paper background** with a winding tan/brown footprint-covered path leading up to a large yellow star at the top. "I Can Follow Jesus" is written across the bottom of the page.

![I Can Follow Jesus Path Craft](/sunday-school/crafts/wise-men-path.jpg)

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Colour the template pieces
The template sheet contains:
- **Two star shapes** — colour bright yellow or gold
- **A winding path strip with footprints** — colour tan or brown
- **A title banner** reading "I Can Follow Jesus" — colour as desired

Colour each piece carefully before cutting.

### Step 2 — Cut out the pieces
Cut along the dashed lines on the template. 
> **For children aged 5–6:** The teacher should pre-cut all pieces before class.

### Step 3 — Assemble on blue paper
Lay the blue construction paper in **landscape** (sideways) orientation.
1. Glue the winding path piece from the **bottom centre**, curving up toward the **top centre** of the page.
2. Glue the **large star** at the top of the path, as if the path leads directly to it.

### Step 4 — Add the title
Glue the **"I Can Follow Jesus"** title banner across the **bottom** of the blue paper, below the path.

### Step 5 — Optional stars
Use a white crayon, white gel pen, or small star stickers to add extra stars scattered around the blue background — just like a night sky.

---

## Discussion While Crafting

As children work, ask:

- *"The wise men followed the star for a very long journey. What helped them keep going?"*
- *"Who were they looking for?"*
- *"If Jesus is the King of Kings, is He worth following?"*
- *"The path in your craft winds and turns — it doesn't go in a straight line. Does following Jesus always go the way we expect?"*

---

## Craft Connection

Point to the finished craft and explain:

> *"This path reminds us of the wise men's journey. They didn't know exactly how long it would take or what every step would look like — but they kept following. The star led them all the way to Jesus. And when they found Him, they worshipped. We can follow Jesus too — one step at a time."*
