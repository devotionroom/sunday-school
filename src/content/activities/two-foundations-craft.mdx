### Purpose
To teach children that when we listen to Jesus and obey His words, we are building our lives on an unshakeable foundation that will stand firm in every storm.

### Finished Craft Reference
![Two Foundations Craft](/sunday-school/crafts/wise-foolish-builders.jpg)

### Setup
1. Print the craft template on white cardstock or paper for each child.
2. Provide each child with one sheet of dark blue construction paper (placed horizontally in landscape orientation).
3. Set out crayons, scissors, and glue sticks. Pre-cut pieces along dashed lines for children aged 5–7.

### Instructions
1. **Color the Pieces:**
   - House 1 (straight/sturdy house with red roof, yellow walls, blue windows).
   - House 2 (tilted house with blue roof, orange walls, red door).
   - The large Rock foundation (grey or dark brown).
   - The Sand slope foundation (yellow or light brown).
   - The dark storm clouds and rain streaks.
   - The banner: *"Build Your Life on Jesus!"*
2. **Cut Out:** Carefully cut out the two houses, two foundations, clouds, rain streaks, and title banner along the dashed lines.
3. **Glue the Foundations:** Glue the Rock on the bottom-left of the blue paper. Glue the Sand on the bottom-right.
4. **Glue the Houses:**
   - Glue House 1 standing straight and firm directly on top of the Rock.
   - Glue House 2 tilted and sliding down into the Sand.
5. **Add Storm & Rain:** Glue the rain clouds at the top above each house. Glue the rain streaks coming down from the clouds (or draw extra white/light blue rain streaks with a crayon!).
6. **Add the Title Banner:** Glue the *"Build Your Life on Jesus!"* banner centered across the top.

### Discussion While Crafting
- *"Why did the house on the rock stay standing when the rain poured down?"*
- *"Did the storm hit both houses or just one of them?"*
- *"What does it mean to build our lives on Jesus when we are at school or home?"*
