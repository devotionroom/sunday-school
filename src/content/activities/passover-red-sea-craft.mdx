### Purpose

To give children a tangible, visual reminder of God's miraculous rescue at the Red Sea. Children create a layered scene showing the parted waters with churning blue waves on both sides, a clear path of dry ground down the middle, and God's people walking through safely under the banner: **"GOD MAKES A WAY!"**

### The Finished Craft

A blue horizontal sheet of paper transformed into the parted Red Sea! The left and right sides are covered with swirling wave patterns drawn in light blue, dark blue, and white chalk or crayon. Running straight down the centre is a clean white strip representing the dry sea floor. Two colourful people figures (a man and a woman/child) walk safely along the dry path, topped by the banner **"GOD MAKES A WAY!"**

### Template & Materials

The printed template (`/sunday-school/printables/passover-red-sea-template.jpg`) includes:
- A title banner: **"GOD MAKES A WAY!"**
- A tall, narrow **"DRY PATH"** rectangle
- Two person-shaped figures (a man and a woman/child) walking forward

Print one template per child on sturdy white cardstock.

### Instructions

1. **Draw the Waves:** Place your blue construction paper horizontally (landscape orientation). Using shades of blue, light blue, and dark blue crayons or markers, draw swirling wave crests across the left and right sides of the paper. Add white crayon or chalk highlights along the edges of the waves to look like splashing foam. Leave a vertical strip down the very centre relatively clear for the dry path.
2. **Colour the People Figures:** On the printed template, colour the two people figures in vibrant colours using crayons or colored pencils (e.g., red, orange, green, brown).
3. **Cut Out the Template Pieces:** Carefully cut out the title banner ("GOD MAKES A WAY!"), the tall narrow Dry Path rectangle, and the two people figures along the dashed lines. *(For children aged 4–6, the teacher should pre-cut these pieces before class.)*
4. **Glue the Dry Path:** Apply glue to the back of the white Dry Path rectangle and press it vertically right down the centre of the blue paper, dividing the waves on the left from the waves on the right.
5. **Glue the People on the Path:** Glue the two people figures standing in the centre of the Dry Path, showing them journeying through the sea on dry ground!
6. **Attach the Title Banner:** Glue the **"GOD MAKES A WAY!"** banner across the top centre of the paper above the dry path.
7. **Admire and Review:** Hold up the finished craft and say together: *"The LORD made a way through the sea!"*

### Discussion While Crafting

Engage the children with these questions while they draw, cut, and glue:

- *"How do you think the Israelites felt when they saw the roaring Red Sea in front of them and Pharaoh's mighty army charging behind them?"* (Scared, trapped, helpless.)
- *"Could any human being push back the sea and dry up the seabed?"* (No! Only God!)
- *"What did God do when there seemed to be no way out?"* (He parted the waters with a strong east wind and made a dry highway through the deep.)
- *"What does this teach us about what God can do in impossible situations?"* (God is faithful and all-powerful; when there seems to be no way, God makes a way!)

<AgeGroup level="preschool">
### For Younger Children

- **Pre-cut everything:** Pre-cut the banner, path strip, and people figures so preschoolers can focus on drawing waves, colouring people, and gluing.
- **Wave making:** Encourage vigorous, joyful scribble-waves! Hand them white chalk and blue crayons and let them draw big swirling ocean water on both sides.
- **Simple repetition:** As they glue the people on the dry path, repeat together: *"God parts the sea! God makes a way!"*
</AgeGroup>

<AgeGroup level="upper-elementary">
### For Older Children

- **Detailed Waves & Walls:** Challenge older students to draw towering walls of water on both sides of the dry path, capturing the biblical description in Exodus 14:22: *"the waters were a wall to them on their right hand and on their left."*
- **Scripture Reflection:** Have them write Exodus 14:14 or Exodus 14:21 in small letters along the edge of the path: *"The LORD will fight for you; you need only to be still."*
- **Deeper Connection:** Discuss how the Red Sea crossing was the final, decisive deliverance from Egypt — just as Christ's resurrection is our complete and final victory over sin and death.
</AgeGroup>
