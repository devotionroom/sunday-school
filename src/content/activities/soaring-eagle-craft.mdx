### Purpose
To give children a physical reminder of Isaiah 40:31, helping them remember that when they wait on the Lord, He gives them the strength to soar like eagles.

### Setup
![Finished Eagle Craft](/sunday-school/crafts/eagle-craft.jpg)

1. Cut out a small white paper "head" shape and a small yellow paper "beak" for each child (or have older children cut their own).
2. Set out the toilet paper rolls, paper, and glue at the tables.

### Instructions
1. **Make the Body:** Have the children take their toilet paper roll and glue brown construction paper around it to cover it completely. 
2. **Make the Wings:** Have the children place both of their hands flat on a piece of brown construction paper. Trace their hands with a marker. (You may need volunteers to help the younger children trace).
3. Cut out the two handprints. These are the eagle's wings!
4. **Assemble the Eagle:** 
   - Glue the handprint wings to the back of the toilet paper roll so they spread out wide on either side.
   - Glue the white head to the top front of the roll. 
   - Glue the yellow beak and googly eyes onto the white head.
5. **Add the Promise:** Have older children write (or help younger children write) *"Isaiah 40:31"* on one wing, and *"Wait on the LORD"* on the other wing. 

### Discussion While Crafting
- *"How high do you think an eagle can fly? They can fly above the storm clouds!"*
- *"When we are tired or frustrated because we have to wait, what does God promise to give us if we wait on Him?"* (Strength to soar).
