type: "Active Game / Opening Activity"
targetAges: ["preschool", "elementary"]
time: "10 minutes"
materials:
  - "Yellow star cut-outs or sticky notes (1 per child)"

## Overview

Before class, hide a simple paper star at the "destination" (a picture of a baby or manger scene). Give children star-shaped clues (or verbal directions from the teacher) that lead from one spot in the room to the next. Each clue says "one step closer!"

When children arrive at the final star and the hidden picture, the teacher reveals the connection: the wise men followed a real star across a great distance to find Jesus.

The question is: **"Are you willing to follow where God leads, even when you don't know exactly where it will end?"**

---

## Setup

1. Before class, tape a simple trail of paper stars around the room (5–8 stars leading from the door to the destination).
2. At the final destination, tape a picture of baby Jesus, a manger scene, or a small nativity figure.
3. Keep the destination covered or out of sight until children arrive there.

---

## Instructions

### For Preschoolers
Tape stars on the floor in a trail and walk the trail **together as a group**. The teacher holds children's hands or leads a conga-line walk from star to star, counting each one out loud. When you arrive at the final star and picture, celebrate: *"You found Him!"*

### For Older Children (Elementary)
Use written clue cards at each star. Each card has a simple directional clue to the next star:
- *"Look near the window."*
- *"Check by the door."*
- *"Somewhere low and close to the floor…"*

Children move as a group, reading clues together, until they reach the destination.

---

## Discussion After the Activity

Once children arrive at the final star:

1. *"The wise men followed a real star — a light in the sky — for a very long journey. How did they know where to go?"*
2. *"They didn't have a map. They didn't know exactly how long it would take. But they kept following."*
3. *"The wise men were looking for a King. And when they found Him — in a house, with His mother Mary — they bowed down and worshipped."*
4. **Big question:** *"Are you willing to follow where God leads, even when you don't know exactly where it will end?"*

---

## Clean-Up

Collect stars from the floor/walls before the lesson begins, or leave them up as a visual reminder throughout class.
