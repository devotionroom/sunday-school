### Purpose
To create a physical reminder of God's provision, tying into the widow's jars of flour and oil that never ran empty.

### Setup
![Finished Blessing Jar](/crafts/blessing-jar.jpeg)

1. Pre-cut colorful paper strips of paper (about 1 inch wide and 4 inches long) so there are plenty for each child.
2. Have the decorating supplies laid out at the tables.

### Instructions
1. **Decorate the Jar:** Give each child a clear plastic cup or small jar. Let them decorate the outside using stickers, permanent markers, or colorful washi tape.
2. **The Blessings:** Give each child 3-5 strips of paper. Have them write or draw something God has provided for them on each strip. (Examples: *My family, food to eat, a bed to sleep in, a warm coat, my church*).
3. **Fill the Jar:** Have them fold the strips of paper and drop them into their beautifully decorated jar.
4. **The Challenge:** Tell the children to take the jar home. Whenever they are feeling worried or sad, they can pull out a strip of paper to remember how God provides for them!

### Discussion While Crafting
- *"How much flour and oil did the widow have left when Elijah asked her for bread?"* (Only enough for one tiny meal).
- *"Did she say no?"* (She obeyed and trusted God).
- *"What happened to the jars after she made the bread?"* (They never ran out! God kept filling them up).
- *"God doesn't always drop bread from the sky, but He gives us everything we need. What are some things you are thankful for today?"*
