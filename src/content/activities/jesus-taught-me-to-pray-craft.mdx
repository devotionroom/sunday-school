### Purpose
To remind children that Jesus gave His followers a clear, beautiful pattern for prayer that teaches us to praise God, seek His will, ask for daily needs, receive and extend forgiveness, and ask for protection.

### Finished Craft Reference
![Jesus Taught Me to Pray Craft](/sunday-school/crafts/lords-prayer.jpg)

### Setup
1. Print the craft template on white paper or cardstock for each child.
2. Provide each child with one sheet of blue construction paper (placed vertically in portrait orientation).
3. Set out crayons, scissors, and glue sticks. For children aged 5–7, pre-cut the pieces along dashed lines.

### Instructions
1. **Color All Pieces:**
   - The large title banner: *"JESUS TAUGHT ME TO PRAY!"* (color in bright rainbow colors).
   - The central Lord's Prayer scroll (yellow/parchment background with pastel border).
   - The Five Prayer Symbols:
     - **PRAISE:** Smiling red heart with sunshine and colorful music notes.
     - **GOD'S WILL:** Green path leading to a brown cross with blue sky.
     - **DAILY NEEDS:** Brown loaf of bread and blue bowl on yellow plate.
     - **FORGIVENESS:** Red heart with white cross and two hands shaking in friendship.
     - **PROTECTION:** Red and gold cross shield, blue umbrella, and red brick house.
2. **Cut Out:** Carefully cut around the banner, the scripture scroll, and the five symbols along the dashed lines.
3. **Glue the Banner:** Glue the *"JESUS TAUGHT ME TO PRAY!"* banner across the top of the blue paper.
4. **Glue the Scripture Scroll:** Glue the Lord's Prayer scroll in the center of the construction paper.
5. **Glue the Five Symbols:**
   - Glue **PRAISE** in the upper-left corner beside the banner.
   - Glue **GOD'S WILL** in the upper-right corner beside the banner.
   - Glue **DAILY NEEDS** on the bottom-left.
   - Glue **FORGIVENESS** on the bottom-center.
   - Glue **PROTECTION** on the bottom-right.
6. **Sign Your Work:** Have each child write their name and age at the bottom (e.g., *"Sam, Age 6"*).

### Discussion While Crafting
- *"What does it mean to 'praise' God when we start our prayers?"*
- *"Why did Jesus say 'daily bread' instead of a whole year of bread?"*
- *"Why is it important to forgive other people when we pray?"*
