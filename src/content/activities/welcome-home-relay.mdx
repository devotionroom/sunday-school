### Purpose
To give children a physical, memorable demonstration of how heavy and exhausting it is to wander away in our own mistakes, and the joy of putting down our burdens and being welcomed into God's family with the "robe" of forgiveness.

### Setup
1. Mark a starting line on one side of the room called the **"Distant Country"**.
2. Mark a turnaround line on the other side called the **"Father's House"**.
3. Place a chair at the Father's House with the bright "Best Robe" laid across it.
4. Have a small backpack loaded with a few books placed at the starting line.

### Instructions
1. Divide children into two equal relay teams.
2. The first runner puts on the heavy backpack ("carrying their own mistakes and burdens").
3. When the teacher calls "Go!", the runner travels from the Distant Country across the room to the Father's House.
4. When they reach the Father's House, they take off the heavy backpack, leave it on the floor, and put on the bright colorful robe ("The Best Robe").
5. The runner takes off the robe, places it back on the chair, runs back to high-five the next teammate, and passes the backpack.
6. The relay continues until all children have experienced leaving the heavy backpack at home and putting on the celebratory robe.

### Age Adaptations
<AgeGroup level="preschool">
For younger children, do not time the game or emphasize winning. Instead, walk through the course together as a group. Have each child carry a small backpack for just a few steps, set it down at the teacher's feet ("the Father"), receive a warm high-five or hug, and put on the colorful robe while the class cheers: *"Welcome Home!"*
</AgeGroup>

<AgeGroup level="upper-elementary">
For older children, add a quick question card at the Father's House station (e.g., *"What were the three gifts the father gave his son?"* or *"What did the son say when he came to his senses?"*). They must answer the question before putting on the robe and running back.
</AgeGroup>
