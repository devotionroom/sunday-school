### Purpose
To remind children that God loves us with an unconditional, welcoming love and is always ready to forgive us and welcome us home when we turn back to Him.

### Finished Craft Reference
![The Father Welcomes His Son](/sunday-school/crafts/prodigal-son.jpg)

### Setup
1. Print the craft template on white paper or cardstock for each child.
2. Provide each child with one sheet of light blue construction paper (placed vertically in portrait orientation).
3. Set out crayons, scissors, and glue sticks. For children aged 5–7, pre-cut the pieces along the dashed lines.

### Instructions
1. **Color the Pieces:** Color the house, the father and son embrace, the winding pathway, the title banner *"GOD WELCOMES ME!"*, and the small stars.
2. **Cut Out:** Carefully cut out the house, pathway, father and son, and title banner along the dashed cut lines.
3. **Glue the House:** Glue the house near the top-left area of the light blue construction paper.
4. **Glue the Pathway:** Glue the pathway curving from the front door of the house down toward the bottom of the page.
5. **Glue the Father and Son:** Glue the embracing father and son in the foreground along the path, showing the father joyfully welcoming his son home.
6. **Add the Title Banner & Stars:** Glue the *"GOD WELCOMES ME!"* banner and decorative stars across the top of the paper.

### Discussion While Crafting
- *"How do you think the son felt when he was walking home? Was he worried?"*
- *"What did the father do when he saw him? Did he stay on the porch or run to meet him?"*
- *"What does this story show us about how God responds when we tell Him we are sorry?"*
