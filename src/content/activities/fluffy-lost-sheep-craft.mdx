### Purpose
To give children a cute, tactile craft that helps them remember how precious and valuable they are to Jesus, the Good Shepherd.

### Setup
![Finished Fluffy Sheep](/sunday-school/crafts/sheep-craft.jpg)

1. Cut the white cardstock into an oval shape for the sheep's body.
2. Cut the black construction paper into small rectangles for the sheep's 4 legs, and an oval for the sheep's face.

### Instructions
1. **The Body:** Give each child a white oval and let them cover it with liquid glue. 
2. Have them stick cotton balls all over the glue until the sheep's body is entirely covered and fluffy!
3. **The Legs:** Glue the four black rectangles to the bottom of the body.
4. **The Face:** Glue the black oval to the front of the body. Let them stick on googly eyes (or use white crayons to draw eyes).
5. **The Name:** Have the teacher or the older children write their own name on the back of the sheep. 

### Discussion While Crafting
- *"How many sheep did the shepherd have in the story?"* (100).
- *"When one ran away, did the shepherd say, 'Oh well, I still have 99 left'?"* (No! He left the 99 to search for the one).
- *"Who is the Good Shepherd?"* (Jesus!).
- *"Who are the sheep?"* (We are!).
- *"Jesus knows your name, just like you wrote your name on the back of this sheep. If you ever feel lost, He will come searching for you!"*
