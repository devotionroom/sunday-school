### Purpose
To help children understand that God's Word is like a seed, and we should listen to it, guard our hearts, and let it grow deep roots that produce good fruit.

### Finished Craft Reference
![The Farmer and the Four Soils Craft](/sunday-school/crafts/parable-sower.jpg)

### Setup
1. Print the craft template on white paper or cardstock for each child.
2. Provide each child with one sheet of light blue construction paper (placed horizontally in landscape orientation).
3. Set out crayons, scissors, and glue sticks. For children aged 5–7, pre-cut the pieces along dashed lines.

### Instructions
1. **Color All Pieces:**
   - The Farmer (blue overalls, yellow straw hat, brown shoes).
   - The Four Soil Sections:
     - **The Path:** Tan soil with black and brown birds eating seeds.
     - **Rocky Ground:** Grey rocks with small green wilting sprouts.
     - **Thorny Ground:** Brown dirt filled with dark spiky briars choking green shoots.
     - **Good Soil:** Rich dark brown earth with tall green plants, pink flowers, and golden ears of corn.
   - The Title Banner (*"Let God's Word Grow in Your Heart!"* in colorful tones).
   - The Soil Name Labels: *The Path, Rocky Ground, Thorny Ground, Good Soil*.
2. **Cut Out:** Carefully cut out the farmer, the four soil panels, the soil labels, and the banner along the dashed lines.
3. **Glue the Soil Labels:** Glue the label strip (*The Path, Rocky Ground, Thorny Ground, Good Soil*) across the bottom edge of the paper.
4. **Glue the Four Soils:** Glue each soil panel directly above its matching name label across the lower half of the page.
5. **Glue the Farmer:** Glue the farmer on the left side, showing him joyfully scattering seeds across the field.
6. **Add the Title Banner:** Glue the *"Let God's Word Grow in Your Heart!"* banner across the top center.

### Discussion While Crafting
- *"Why didn't the seed grow on the hard path? What happened to it?"*
- *"What made the plants die on the rocky ground?"*
- *"What kinds of 'weeds' or worries can distract us from God's Word?"*
- *"How can our hearts be like the good soil?"*
