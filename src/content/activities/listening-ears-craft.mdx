### Purpose
To create a fun, wearable craft that reminds children of Samuel's willing heart and his famous response: "Speak, for your servant is listening."

### Setup
![Finished Listening Ears](/sunday-school/crafts/listening-ears.jpeg)

1. Cut construction paper into long strips (you will need to staple two strips together to fit around a child's head).
2. Cut the paper plates (or cardstock) into giant, oversized ear shapes. You need two giant ears per child.

### Instructions
1. **Decorate:** Give each child a pair of giant paper ears and let them decorate them however they like.
2. **The Verse:** On one ear, have them write *"Speak, LORD."* On the other ear, have them write *"I am listening."*
3. **Assemble:** The teacher should staple or tape the giant ears to the sides of the construction paper headband.
4. **Wear it:** Wrap the headband around the child's head, measure it so it fits snugly, and staple it closed.
5. **Practice:** Have all the children put on their giant listening ears. Whisper instructions to them from across the room (e.g., *"Touch your nose,"* *"Spin in a circle"*) to test how well their giant ears work!

### Discussion While Crafting
- *"How many times did God call Samuel's name before Samuel figured out who it was?"* (Three times!).
- *"Who did Samuel think was calling him at first?"* (Eli).
- *"When God speaks to us today, is it usually a loud booming voice, or a quiet whisper in our heart?"* (A quiet whisper, or through reading the Bible).
- *"When you wear these giant ears, remember that God wants you to be ready to listen to Him every single day."*
