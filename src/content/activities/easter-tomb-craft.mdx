### Purpose
To give children a vibrant, three-dimensional visual reminder of the resurrection. By rolling the stone away to reveal an empty tomb surrounded by blooming flowers, children celebrate the historical truth that Jesus is alive.

### The Template
The printed template contains:
- A large circle scene (**Plate Base**) showing the hillside and tomb outline for colouring, with the words **"Jesus Is Alive!"**
- A **Stone** to cut out (a large rock shape)
- **Flowers & Leaves** (multiple spring flower and leaf shapes to cut out and layer around the tomb)

### Finished Craft Reference
![Jesus Is Alive! Tomb Craft](/sunday-school/crafts/easter-tomb.jpg)

The finished craft is made on a white paper plate with the tomb scene coloured directly on the plate (or glued from the template circle). The sky is coloured blue at the top, the hill and ground green and brown, and the arched tomb opening is dark black. The stone has been rolled to the right side of the opening. Colourful flowers and fresh leaves are glued around the base, and "Jesus Is Alive!" arches across the top in bright yellow/orange letters.

### Setup
Print one "Jesus Is Alive! Tomb Craft Template" per child on sturdy white cardstock. Provide one large white paper plate per child. Cut small arched pieces of black construction paper or cardstock for the inside of the tomb opening if children prefer paper collage over black crayon. For children aged 5–6, pre-cut the stone, flowers, and leaves before class to save time.

### Instructions
1. **Colour the Plate Base circle scene:** Colour the sky blue across the top half of the circle. Colour the ground and hillside rich green and earthy brown. Colour the letters of "Jesus Is Alive!" bright yellow or warm orange. Colour the inside of the arched tomb opening completely black with a crayon, or glue on a pre-cut piece of black paper to represent the dark, empty tomb interior.
2. **Cut out the template pieces:** Carefully cut out the Stone and the Flowers & Leaves shapes along their outer outlines. Teacher should pre-cut these for preschoolers and kindergarteners.
3. **Glue the circle scene to the plate:** Cut out the large circular scene from the template and glue it securely to the centre of the white paper plate using a glue stick. (Alternatively, the scene may be transferred or drawn directly onto the paper plate).
4. **Glue the rolled-away Stone:** Glue the cut-out stone just to the right side of the dark tomb opening — positioned as though an angel has rolled it completely aside!
5. **Colour and glue the Flowers:** Colour the flowers vibrant pink, bright red, and sunny yellow; colour the leaves fresh green. Glue them in a cheerful garden cluster along the bottom and sides of the hillside around the tomb entrance.
6. **Display and Celebrate:** Have every child hold up their finished plate and proclaim together with joyful voices: **"Jesus Is Alive!"**

<AgeGroup level="preschool">
### For Younger Children
Pre-cut all small pieces (the stone, flowers, and leaves) and pre-glue the black tomb opening onto the template before class. Let young children focus on colouring the sky, hill, and flowers with broad strokes. Point out the dark cave and the stone on the side: *"Look! The stone is rolled away and Jesus is not inside — He is alive!"*
</AgeGroup>

<AgeGroup level="upper-elementary">
### For Older Children
Encourage older children to add detailed shading to the stone to give it a realistic 3D appearance. They can write the scripture reference **"Luke 24:6"** or **"Matthew 28:6"** inside the empty tomb or along the bottom rim of the plate: *"He is not here; He has risen!"*
</AgeGroup>

### Discussion While Crafting
- *"Why was the stone rolled away from the tomb?"* (Not to let Jesus out, but to show everyone that He was already risen!)
- *"What did the angel say to the women who came to the tomb on Sunday morning?"* ("He is not here; He has risen, just as He said!")
- *"If Jesus rose bodily from the dead, what does that mean for each of us today?"* (He truly paid for our sins, conquered death forever, and gives us eternal life!)
