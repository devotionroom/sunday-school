## Finished Craft

A glowing candle scene on a dark blue or purple background. The candle body (white/cream with drips) is glued near the bottom-centre. A large yellow flame and smaller orange flame are layered above it. The text banner "Jesus Is the Light of the World." is glued at the bottom. Optional rays and stars are drawn on the dark background in white/yellow chalk or crayon.

![Jesus Is the Light of the World — Candle Craft](/sunday-school/crafts/hanukkah-candle.jpg)

## Teacher Preparation

- Print the craft template on white cardstock. One sheet per child.
- Pre-cut template pieces for children aged 5–6 before class.
- Set out crayons (yellow, orange, white/cream) and glue sticks.
- Have the dark blue or dark purple construction paper ready, one sheet per child (portrait orientation).
- Optional: set out white/yellow chalk or oil pastels for background decoration.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This craft uses a battery-operated candle or drawn/paper candle only. Do NOT use real candles, matches, or any open flame at any point in this activity.

## Instructions

### 1. Prepare the Background
Hold the dark blue or dark purple construction paper **vertically (portrait orientation)**. This is the base of the finished craft.

### 2. Colour the Template Pieces
Using crayons, colour each piece:
- **Candle body** — Colour white or cream (press firmly so the colour is bright and the drip details show).
- **Large flame (teardrop shape)** — Colour bright yellow all the way to the edges.
- **Small flame (smaller teardrop)** — Colour orange.
- **Text banner ("Jesus Is the Light of the World.")** — Colour the border yellow or gold.

### 3. Cut Out All Pieces
Cut out the candle body, large flame, small flame, and text banner along the dashed lines.
*Teacher pre-cuts for children aged 5–6.*

### 4. Draw Rays and Stars on the Background (Optional)
Using yellow or white crayon, chalk, or oil pastel, draw lines radiating outward from the top-centre of the dark paper (like rays of light), and add small stars scattered across the background. This creates a beautiful glowing night-sky effect.

### 5. Glue the Candle
Apply glue to the back of the candle body piece and press it near the **bottom-centre** of the dark paper.

### 6. Glue the Large Yellow Flame
Apply glue to the back of the large yellow flame and press it **directly above the candle wick**, overlapping slightly.

### 7. Glue the Small Orange Flame
Apply glue to the small orange flame and press it **in the centre of the yellow flame**, creating a layered, glowing effect.

### 8. Glue the Text Banner
Apply glue to the back of the text banner and press it along the **bottom of the page**, below the candle.

### 9. Done!
Hold up the finished craft and read the words together: *"Jesus is the light of the world."*

## Discussion While Crafting

As children work, ask:
- *"Why do you think the people were so happy to have the Temple rededicated?"*
- *"Jesus said He is the light of the world. What do you think that means?"*
- *"How does Jesus bring light into dark places in our lives?"*

## Age Adaptation

**Preschool:** Pre-cut all pieces and have children focus on colouring and gluing. Let them scribble "rays" on the background with big yellow strokes. The finished art does not need to look perfect — the conversation is what matters.

**Elementary:** Encourage neat colouring and careful layering of the flames. Older children can also add more detail to the background (stars, a gradient effect with different shades). Challenge them to write John 8:12 on the back of their craft from memory.
