### Purpose
To remind children how four faithful friends brought a paralyzed man to Jesus and how Jesus had the divine power and authority to both heal his body and forgive his sins.

### Finished Craft Reference
![Through the Roof Craft](/sunday-school/crafts/paralyzed-man-roof.jpg)

### Setup
1. Print the craft template on white cardstock or sturdy paper for each child.
2. Provide each child with one sheet of light blue construction paper (placed horizontally in landscape orientation).
3. Set out crayons, scissors, and glue sticks.
4. For younger children (ages 5–7), pre-cut or slit the center rectangular area marked *"CUT OUT / REMOVE CENTER"* on the house so the inner room is open.

### Instructions
1. **Color All Pieces:**
   - The House (brown/tan clay walls with darker brown wooden beams and doors).
   - The Four Friends (Friend 1, 2, 3, and 4 peering over the roof).
   - Jesus (white tunic with bright red sash).
   - The Man on his Mat (showing the four ropes).
   - The Title Banner (*"Jesus Has the Power to Forgive!"*).
2. **Cut Out:** Carefully cut around the house, four friends, Jesus, the man on the mat, and the title banner along the dashed lines.
3. **Open the Roof/Room:** Cut along the dashed line around the inner front section labeled *"CUT OUT / REMOVE CENTER"* and discard it, leaving the interior room open.
4. **Glue the House:** Glue the house in the center of the light blue construction paper.
5. **Glue Jesus:** Glue Jesus standing inside the room opening near the bottom center.
6. **Glue the Four Friends:** Glue the four friends along the top roof ledge, peering down through the roof opening.
7. **Glue the Man on the Mat:** Glue the man on his mat in the upper-middle of the room opening, suspended by ropes as though his four friends are lowering him down to Jesus.
8. **Add the Title Banner:** Glue the *"Jesus Has the Power to Forgive!"* banner along the bottom edge of the paper.

### Discussion While Crafting
- *"Why did the four friends carry their friend up to the roof?"*
- *"Did the crowd let them through the front door? Did that stop them?"*
- *"What did Jesus say was the man's greatest need? (His sins forgiven!)"*
