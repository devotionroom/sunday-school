### Purpose
To give children a fun, interactive prop they can use to recreate the silent march and the loud shout that brought down the walls of Jericho.

### Setup
![Finished Jericho Trumpet](/crafts/jericho-trumpet.jpeg)

1. Pre-cut the crepe paper streamers into 6-inch lengths.
2. Have the paper tubes and decorating supplies ready at the tables.

### Instructions
1. **The Horn:** Give each child a cardboard tube. This will be their "ram's horn" or trumpet!
2. **Decorate:** Let them color and decorate the outside of the tube however they want using markers, crayons, or stickers.
3. **The Tassels:** Have the children take 3 or 4 strips of the crepe paper streamers and tape them to the *inside* of one end of the tube. When they hold the tube up to blow into it, the colorful streamers will hang down!
4. **The March:** Have the children line up holding their trumpets. Tell them they have to march around the room one time in *absolute silence*. 
5. **The Shout:** When they finish their silent lap, the teacher should yell, *"BLOW THE TRUMPETS!"* Have all the children blow into their cardboard tubes and give a massive shout!

### Discussion While Crafting
- *"Did the Israelites have giant cannons or catapults to break down the walls of Jericho?"* (No, they just had trumpets and a promise from God).
- *"Was it hard for the Israelites to march in complete silence?"* (Probably! But they had to obey God's instructions exactly).
- *"Who really knocked the walls down?"* (God did, because the people obeyed Him!).
