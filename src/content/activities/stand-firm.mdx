**Type:** Movement / Discussion Activity  
**Time:** 10–15 minutes  
**Group Size:** 3+  
**Materials:** None  

### Purpose
Help children practice identifying choices that require faithfulness.

### Setup
Choose one side of the room as: **STAND FIRM**  
Choose the opposite side as: **GIVE IN**

Explain:  
*"I'm going to describe situations. Move to the side that shows what you think you should do."*

### Scenario 1
A friend tells you:  
*"Don't tell the teacher we broke it. Just say you don't know what happened."*  
What should you do?  
**Stand Firm:** Tell the truth.  
Discuss why.

### Scenario 2
Your friends are making fun of another child.  
They expect you to laugh.  
What should you do?  
**Stand Firm:** Don't join in.  
Discuss what kindness might look like.

### Scenario 3
You know you're supposed to pray, but someone says:  
*"That's weird. Don't do that."*  
What should you do?  
**Stand Firm:** Continue following God.

### Scenario 4
You know the right answer on a test isn't yours because someone showed it to you.  
Everyone else is using it.  
What should you do?  
**Stand Firm:** Be honest.

### Scenario 5
You tell the truth and now you're worried you might get in trouble.  
Was telling the truth still the right choice?  
**Yes.**  
Explain:  
*"Doing right doesn't always mean everything immediately becomes easy."*

### Connect It to Daniel
Ask:  
*"What did Daniel do when doing what was right became difficult?"*  
**He stayed faithful to God.**

Say:  
**"Daniel stood firm before he ever entered the lions' den."**  
His courage began with ordinary faithfulness.
