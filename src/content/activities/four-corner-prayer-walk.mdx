### Purpose
To guide children practically through the five core elements of the Lord's Prayer, transforming prayer from an abstract concept into an active, conversational encounter with God.

### Setup
1. Post one sign in each of the four corners of your classroom:
   - **Corner 1: PRAISE** (*"Hallowed be Your Name"*)
   - **Corner 2: GOD'S WILL** (*"Your Kingdom Come, Your Will Be Done"*)
   - **Corner 3: DAILY NEEDS** (*"Give Us This Day Our Daily Bread"*)
   - **Corner 4: FORGIVENESS & PROTECTION** (*"Forgive Us... Deliver Us from Evil"*)
2. Place slips of paper and markers at each corner.

### Instructions
1. Divide the class into four small groups and assign each group to start at a different corner.
2. Spend 2–3 minutes at each corner before rotating clockwise:
   - **At PRAISE:** Children whisper or write one thing they love about God (e.g., *"You are kind," "You made mountains"*).
   - **At GOD'S WILL:** Children pray for missionaries, pastors, and leaders, asking God's love to spread across the earth.
   - **At DAILY NEEDS:** Children draw or write one need they or their family has today (food, healing, peace) and place it in the basket.
   - **At FORGIVENESS & PROTECTION:** Children quietly pray: *"Father, forgive my mistakes, and help me forgive others."* They place a small white bead or paper into a bowl of clear water to symbolize clean hearts.
3. Bring the entire class back together in a circle to close in the Lord's Prayer.

### Age Adaptations
<AgeGroup level="preschool">
Walk the whole class together from corner to corner. At each corner, lead them in a simple one-sentence prayer and motion (praise = clapping hands; needs = open cupped hands; forgiveness = hand over heart).
</AgeGroup>

<AgeGroup level="upper-elementary">
Encourage students to write specific personal prayer requests on the slips at the Needs station and pray over each other's slips in pairs.
</AgeGroup>
