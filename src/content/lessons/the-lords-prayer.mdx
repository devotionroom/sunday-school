## Teacher Preparation

**Before class:**
- Read Matthew 6:5–15 and Luke 11:1–13.
- Notice Jesus' contrast: the Pharisees stood on street corners praying theatrical prayers to win human applause. Jesus said the Father hears the quiet whispers behind closed doors.
- Prepare the four station signs for *The Four-Corner Prayer Walk*.
- Print the *Jesus Taught Me to Pray!* activity template and prepare blue construction paper for each child.
- Write the Lord's Prayer on the board so children can follow along.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **A note for the teacher**
> Emphasize that the Lord's Prayer was not given as a mechanical chant or magical formula. Jesus was providing a **pattern** or roadmap for a child's prayer life:
> 1. Praise God's Name.
> 2. Seek God's Will.
> 3. Trust for Daily Needs.
> 4. Forgive and Be Forgiven.
> 5. Ask for Spiritual Protection.

## Opening: How Do You Talk to Someone You Love?

Ask the children:
*"When you talk to your mom, dad, or best friend, do you use fancy, poetic language like you are giving a speech on television? Or do you just talk normally, sharing what happened and what you need?"*

Allow 1–2 children to respond.

Explain:
*"You talk normally because you know they love you! In Bible times, some people thought prayer meant using long, impressive words so people on the street would applaud. But Jesus taught that God is our loving Father in heaven, and He wants us to come to Him with honest, humble hearts."*

## The Bible Story: Matthew 6:5–15

### 1. How NOT to Pray
Jesus noticed that some people turned prayer into an acting audition. They stood on street corners in bright robes, praying with booming, dramatic voices so everyone walking by would say, *"Look how holy they are!"* 

Jesus warned His followers:
> *"And when you pray, do not be like the hypocrites. For they love to pray standing in the synagogues and on the street corners to be seen by men... But when you pray, go into your inner room, close your door, and pray to your Father who is in secret."* (Matthew 6:5–6, BSB)

God doesn't grade our prayers on our vocabulary. He looks at the sincerity of our hearts!

### 2. "Our Father in Heaven, Hallowed Be Your Name"
Then Jesus gave His disciples the greatest pattern for prayer ever spoken:
> *"This, then, is how you should pray:"*
> *"Our Father in heaven, hallowed be Your name..."* (Matthew 6:9, BSB)

Notice the very first word: **Father**. We are not praying to an uncaring boss or a distant cloud; we are speaking to our heavenly Father who adopted us! 

And His name is **hallowed**—which means holy, sacred, and worthy of our highest praise. Prayer doesn't start with our shopping list of wants; it starts by remembering how great and holy God is!

### 3. "Your Kingdom Come, Your Will Be Done"
Next, Jesus taught us to pray:
> *"Your kingdom come, Your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven."* (Matthew 6:10, BSB)

In heaven, the angels obey God immediately, joyfully, and completely. When we pray this, we are saying: *"Lord, I want what You want more than what I want. Rule in my school, my home, and my heart today just like You rule heaven."*

### 4. "Give Us This Day Our Daily Bread"
Then Jesus invites us to bring our physical needs:
> *"Give us this day our daily bread."* (Matthew 6:11, BSB)

Notice Jesus didn't say, "Give us bread for the next ten years!" Just like God sent manna to the Israelites one morning at a time in the desert, He wants us to trust Him today for today's food, strength, comfort, and peace. We don't have to worry about tomorrow, because God is already there!

### 5. "And Forgive Us Our Debts, as We Forgive Our Debtors"
Jesus continued to the health of our souls:
> *"And forgive us our debts, as we also have forgiven our debtors."* (Matthew 6:12, BSB)

Just as our bodies need bread every single day, our souls need God's cleansing forgiveness every single day. And when we realize how much God has forgiven us, we cannot hold anger against our siblings or classmates. We must be quick to forgive!

### 6. "And Lead Us Not into Temptation, but Deliver Us from Evil"
Finally, Jesus taught us to pray for safety:
> *"And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one."* (Matthew 6:13, BSB)

We live in a world with traps, peer pressure, and bad choices. We ask God to guide our steps away from sin and protect us from the devil's lies. God is our Shield and Strong Tower!

## The Deeper Meaning: Three Things to Remember

### 1. Praise Comes Before Petitions
When we start our prayers by praising God for who He is—His holiness, His love, and His power—our problems shrink into their proper perspective.

### 2. Trust God for Today
God provides for us one day at a time. When anxiety whispers, *"What if something goes wrong tomorrow?"*, the Lord's Prayer reminds us: *"Give us this day our daily bread."* Trust God for today!

### 3. Forgiven People Must Be Forgiving People
You cannot hold out an open hand to receive God's forgiveness while keeping your other hand balled into a fist of anger toward someone else. Forgiving others is the proof that you understand God's grace.

## Age Adaptations

<AgeGroup level="preschool">
### Younger Children (Ages 5–7)
- **Focus:** The Five-Finger Prayer:
  - **Thumb (points to you):** Praise God!
  - **Pointer (points the way):** God's will and kingdom.
  - **Tall Finger (tallest):** Leaders, parents, teachers.
  - **Ring Finger (weakest):** Sick friends and daily needs.
  - **Pinky (smallest):** Ask God to forgive my mistakes.
</AgeGroup>

<AgeGroup level="upper-elementary">
### Older Children (Ages 8–11)
- **Focus:** The radical nature of praying *"Your will be done."*
- **Discussion:** When is it hard to say "Your will be done" instead of "My will be done"? (e.g., when a sports game doesn't go your way, when plans get cancelled, or when God's answer is "wait").
- **Challenge:** How does praying for God's will protect us from our own shortsighted desires?
</AgeGroup>

## Discussion Questions

### Understanding the Story
1. Why did Jesus warn against praying loudly on street corners like hypocrites?
2. What does it mean that God is our "Father in heaven"?
3. What does the word "hallowed" mean?
4. Why did Jesus teach us to ask for "daily bread" instead of bread for a whole year?
5. What did Jesus say we must do when we ask God to forgive our debts/sins?

### Thinking Deeper
6. In heaven, how is God's will done? How can we do God's will on earth at school or home?
7. What is the difference between treating prayer like a magic spell vs. talking to a loving Father?
8. Why is asking for protection against temptation just as important as asking for food?

### Personal Application
9. Where is a quiet place in your house where you can talk to God in secret?
10. Is there someone who offended or hurt you recently that you need to forgive in prayer?
11. What is one daily need you can ask your Heavenly Father for right now?

## Memory Verse

**Matthew 6:9–10 (BSB)**  
> *"Our Father in heaven, hallowed be Your name. Your kingdom come, Your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven."*

### Teaching Method: The Reverent Echo
1. *"Our Father in heaven,"* (reach both hands gently up toward the sky)
2. *"hallowed be Your name."* (bow head with hands folded reverently over heart)
3. *"Your kingdom come,"* (form a crown above head using fingers)
4. *"Your will be done,"* (place hands flat together in front in ready obedience)
5. *"on earth as it is in heaven."* (point down to floor, then sweep hands upward toward heaven!)
Repeat together in a whisper, then at a speaking volume, then in a confident voice! Play the Scripture song during craft time.

## Application

You can pray anytime, anywhere! 
- You don't have to be in a church building.
- You don't have to use Elizabethan old English words like "thee" and "thou."
- You can pray while you ride in the car, while you take a math test, or while you lie in bed at night.

Whenever you pray, remember Jesus' roadmap:
1. **Praise:** *"Father, You are good and holy."*
2. **Will:** *"Help me obey You today."*
3. **Needs:** *"Please give me strength and food for today."*
4. **Forgiveness:** *"Forgive my bad attitude, and help me forgive my brother."*
5. **Protection:** *"Help me say NO to bad choices today."*

---

## Take-Home Challenge

Hang your **"Jesus Taught Me to Pray"** poster right above your bed. Every evening before you go to sleep this week, touch each of the five symbols (Praise, Will, Needs, Forgiveness, Protection) and pray one sentence for each!
