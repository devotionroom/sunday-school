## Teacher Preparation

**Before class:**
- Read Mark 2:1–12 and Luke 5:17–26 carefully.
- Notice the sequence of Jesus' actions: He forgives the man's sins **first**, and only performs the physical healing **second** to prove His divine authority to the skeptical religious scribes.
- Flat roofs in ancient Galilee were made of wooden beams layered with reeds, brushwood, and thick packed clay or tiles. Digging through was noisy, messy, and physically demanding.
- Prepare towels and stuffed animals for the *Four Friends Stretcher Carry* activity.
- Print the *Through the Roof!* template and prepare light blue construction paper.
- Write the memory verse on the board.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **A note for the teacher**
> Be careful not to suggest that sickness is always a direct punishment for a person's individual sins. The man was not paralyzed because he was a worse sinner than the people standing in the room. Rather, Jesus was showing that physical paralysis is a visible picture of the spiritual paralysis caused by sin in every human heart, and He alone holds the power to cure both.

## Opening: A House Packed to the Rims

Ask the children:
*"Have you ever tried to squeeze into a room that was so packed with people that you couldn't take a single step forward? What did you do? Did you push through, or did you give up and walk away?"*

Allow 1–2 responses.

Explain:
*"When Jesus preached in the town of Capernaum, the house was so packed that people were spilling out onto the street and peeking through the windows. Today we will meet four incredible friends who came up with a crazy, daring plan when the front door was blocked!"*

## The Bible Story: Mark 2:1–12

### 1. The Crowded House in Capernaum
Word spread quickly through the streets: *"Jesus is home!"* 

Within hours, so many people rushed to the house that there was no room left anywhere—not in the living area, not in the doorway, not even on the front porch. People were shoulder-to-shoulder, straining to hear every word Jesus preached. Religious leaders called Pharisees and teachers of the law were sitting in front, watching Jesus with sharp, critical eyes.

### 2. Four Friends with a Mission
Down the street came four men carrying a heavy load. Between them, they carried a friend on a sleeping mat. This man was paralyzed—his legs were limp and lifeless, and he could not walk, run, or stand. He had to depend on others for everything.

His four friends loved him dearly. They had heard about Jesus healing the sick and casting out demons, and they believed with all their hearts: *"If we can just get our friend to Jesus, he will be healed!"*

### 3. Blocked at the Door
When the four friends arrived, their hearts sank. A solid wall of people blocked the entrance. They shouted: *"Excuse us! Please let us through! We have a sick friend!"* But no one would budge. People were too focused on seeing Jesus for themselves.

Most people would have given up and gone home. But these four friends were determined!

### 4. Digging Through the Roof
In ancient Israel, houses had flat roofs reached by an outside staircase. People often sat on the roof in the cool of the evening. 

The four men looked at each other, nodded, and began carrying their friend up the narrow outside steps to the roof. Once they were standing above the room where Jesus was speaking, they dropped to their knees and began pulling up the roof tiles, clay, and branches! 

Imagine being inside the room below: suddenly, plaster dust and bits of dried clay start raining down on everyone's heads! The religious leaders must have brushed dust off their fancy robes in irritation. Jesus stopped preaching and looked up as a giant rectangle of blue sky opened in the ceiling!

### 5. "Your Sins Are Forgiven"
The four friends tied ropes to the four corners of the mat. Working together in perfect balance, they slowly lowered their paralyzed friend right down through the hole in the roof, placing him gently at the feet of Jesus!

Mark records something amazing:
> *"When Jesus saw their faith, He said to the paralytic, 'Son, your sins are forgiven.'"* (Mark 2:5, BSB)

Notice that Jesus looked up and saw **their faith**—the active, loving, persistent faith of the four friends! But Jesus' words shocked the room. Everyone expected Him to say, "Walk!" Instead, He dealt with the man's eternal soul: *"Your sins are forgiven."*

### 6. Whispers of Blasphemy
The religious scribes were outraged. They thought in their hearts:
> *"Why does this man speak like this? He is blaspheming! Who can forgive sins but God alone?"* (Mark 2:7, BSB)

They were right about one thing: only God has the right to forgive sins committed against God. But they were blind to the truth that the Son of God was standing right in front of them!

### 7. "Get Up, Pick Up Your Mat, and Go Home"
Jesus knew immediately what they were thinking in their hearts. He confronted them:
> *"Why are you thinking these things in your hearts? Which is easier: to say to the paralytic, 'Your sins are forgiven,' or to say, 'Get up, pick up your mat, and walk'?"* (Mark 2:8–9, BSB)

Think about that question! Anyone can *say* "your sins are forgiven" because no one can see into a person's soul to check. But if you tell a paralyzed man to walk, everyone will instantly see whether you have real power or not!

So Jesus turned to the crowd and declared our memory verse:
> *"But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins..."*
> He said to the paralytic, *"I tell you, get up, pick up your mat, and go home."* (Mark 2:10–11, BSB)

### 8. Walking Out Before Everyone
In front of the stunned scribes, the paralyzed man's legs surged with strength! Feeling returned to his toes, strength filled his knees, and his muscles tightened. He leaped to his feet, rolled up his sleeping mat under his arm, and walked out the door in full view of everyone!

The crowd was overwhelmed with awe. They praised God and cried out:
> *"We have never seen anything like this!"* (Mark 2:12, BSB)

## The Deeper Meaning: Three Things to Remember

### 1. Bring Your Friends to Jesus
The paralyzed man could not get to Jesus on his own. He needed four friends who loved him enough to carry him, climb stairs, and dig through a roof. We can "carry our friends to Jesus" today by praying for them, inviting them to Sunday School, and showing them Christ's love.

### 2. Real Faith Overcomes Obstacles
The four friends didn't quit when the front door was jammed shut. When you pray for someone and nothing seems to happen right away, do not give up. Keep praying, keep caring, and keep trusting Jesus.

### 3. Forgiveness Is Our Greatest Miracle
Having strong legs to run and play is a wonderful blessing. But Jesus knew that the man's greatest problem was sin. When Jesus forgave his sins, He gave him eternal life. Jesus has the power to fix our physical problems and the divine authority to forgive all our guilt.

<AgeGroup level="preschool">
### Younger Children (Ages 5–7)
- **Focus:** Helping hands and Jesus making the man walk.
- **Action:** Have children pretend to hold the four corners of a rope, count *"One, two, three, lower down!"*, and then jump up and cheer when Jesus heals the man!
- **Key Idea:** Jesus loves us, heals our hurts, and forgives our mistakes.
</AgeGroup>

<AgeGroup level="upper-elementary">
### Older Children (Ages 8–11)
- **Focus:** The theological debate over Jesus' identity.
- **Discussion:** Why did the religious scribes call Jesus' words blasphemy? How does this story prove beyond any shadow of a doubt that Jesus is not just a moral teacher or good prophet, but God Himself walking on earth?
</AgeGroup>

## Discussion Questions

### Understanding the Story
1. Why couldn't the four friends get their paralyzed friend inside the house?
2. What bold plan did the four friends carry out instead?
3. What was the very first thing Jesus said to the paralyzed man when he was lowered down?
4. Why were the religious leaders upset by Jesus' words of forgiveness?
5. What physical miracle did Jesus perform to prove He had the power to forgive sins?

### Thinking Deeper
6. Why does the Bible say Jesus saw *"their faith"* (plural)? What does that teach us about friendship?
7. What did Jesus mean by asking: *"Which is easier to say?"*
8. If the man had only received walking legs but never had his sins forgiven, why would he still be in danger?

### Personal Application
9. Who is a friend in your life who needs to know Jesus? How can you "carry their mat" this week?
10. Have you ever asked Jesus to forgive your sins? How does it feel knowing your slate is wiped clean?
11. How can our class be a team of friends who help each other follow Jesus?

## Memory Verse

**Mark 2:10 (BSB)**  
> *"But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins..."*

### Teaching Method: The Four-Corner Recite
Assign four children to stand at the four corners of the classroom holding cards labeled:
1. Corner 1: *"But that you may know"*
2. Corner 2: *"that the Son of Man"*
3. Corner 3: *"has authority on earth"*
4. Corner 4: *"to forgive sins!"*
The class points to Corner 1 and shouts the phrase, then turns to Corner 2, 3, and 4 in a circle around the room. Repeat three times, turning faster each time! Play the Scripture song during craft time.

## Application

You might not have a friend who is paralyzed, but you have friends who are lonely, sad, scared, or don't know that God loves them. 

You can be one of the "four friends" today:
- You can pray for your friends by name every single day.
- You can invite them to come to Sunday School with you.
- You can stand up for a classmate who is being teased on the playground.
- You can tell them: *"Jesus loves you and forgives your mistakes."*

---

## Take-Home Challenge

Take an index card and write the name of **one friend** who needs God's help. Every morning this week before school, hold that card in your hands and pray: *"Lord Jesus, please help my friend and show them Your love today."*

---

## Closing Thought

The four friends brought their friend to Jesus, and Jesus gave him back his legs—and gave his soul eternal life. When you bring your life and your friends to Jesus, He does far more than you could ever ask or imagine.

Give us the faithful love of the four friends. Help us to bring our friends to You in prayer, to never give up when obstacles arise, and to trust that You can do the impossible. In Your holy name, Amen."*
